Ticket Delivery Policy

Information about digital ticket delivery, access, and event entry procedures

Digital Ticket Delivery

TLDS Events operates as a digital event registration platform. All tickets are delivered electronically via email and accessible through your registered account. We do not ship physical tickets or merchandise.

Important: Your digital ticket contains a unique QR code that is required for event entry. Please ensure your email address is correct during registration.

Ticket Delivery Timeline

Tickets are delivered immediately after successful payment confirmation:

  • Instant delivery via email to your registered email address
  • QR code ticket attached as a PDF file
  • Confirmation email with booking details
  • Access to ticket through your account dashboard

Ticket Access & Retrieval

You can access your digital tickets through multiple methods:

  • Email Delivery: Check your registered email inbox for the ticket PDF
  • Account Dashboard: Log in to view and download your tickets
  • Mobile Access: Save the ticket PDF to your mobile device for easy access

Event Entry Procedure

To enter the event, you must present your digital ticket:

  • Display the QR code from your digital ticket at the entrance
  • Our scanner will verify the ticket authenticity
  • Valid ID proof may be required for verification
  • Once scanned, the ticket is marked as used

Ticket Security & Validity

To ensure ticket security and prevent fraud:

  • Each ticket has a unique QR code that can only be used once
  • Tickets are non-transferable and linked to the registered attendee
  • Sharing or selling your ticket QR code may result in invalidation
  • Report lost or stolen tickets immediately to our support team

Delivery Issues & Support

If you experience any issues with ticket delivery:

  • Check your spam/junk folder for the delivery email
  • Ensure the email address provided during registration is correct
  • Contact support if you haven't received your ticket within 10 minutes
  • Include your booking reference number in all communications

Special Circumstances

In case of event changes or cancellations:

  • Updated tickets will be sent automatically for rescheduled events
  • Refund information will be communicated for cancelled events
  • Follow our social media channels for event updates
  • Contact support for any event-related queries
