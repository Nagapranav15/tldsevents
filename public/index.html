About The Event


Tollywood Mehfil is an electrifying jamming session that brings together music lovers, performers, and enthusiasts to celebrate the vibrant spirit of Telugu cinema. This event is all about raw talent, live energy, and unforgettable vibes, where participants can sing, play instruments, and relive iconic Tollywood melodies in a lively, interactive setting. From classic hits to trending chartbusters, the mehfil creates a space for creativity, collaboration, and pure musical passion, making it a must-attend experience for anyone who lives and breathes Tollywood music.

About The Band


Eira The Band is a dynamic multi-city music collective known for delivering high-energy live performances and soulful musical experiences. With a unique concept of “one name, multiple bands” performing across cities like Bengaluru and Hyderabad, they bring together diverse artists under a single identity. Their style blends live jamming, Sufi vibes, and contemporary music, creating an immersive atmosphere that connects deeply with audiences. From hosting open jam sessions to performing at events and cultural nights, Eira focuses on spreading emotion and energy through music, making every performance feel interactive, raw, and memorable.
