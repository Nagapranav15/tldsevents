Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

Thinklab Digital Solutions LLP

1. Cancellation Policy

We understand that plans may change. Our cancellation policy is as follows:

  • 30+ days before event: Full refund (100%)
  • 15-29 days before event: 75% refund
  • 7-14 days before event: 50% refund
  • Less than 7 days before event: No refund

2. How to Request a Cancellation

To request a cancellation and refund:

  • Contact us via email at info@thinklabdigitalsolutions.com
  • Provide your booking ID and registered email address
  • Include the reason for cancellation
  • Refunds will be processed within 7-10 business days

3. Refund Process

Once your cancellation request is approved:

  • Refunds will be credited to the original payment method
  • Processing time: 7-10 business days
  • You will receive a confirmation email when refund is initiated
  • Bank holidays may delay processing

4. Non-Refundable Situations

Note: Refunds will NOT be provided in the following situations:

  • Cancellations requested less than 7 days before the event
  • No-shows (failure to attend the event without prior cancellation)
  • Duplicate bookings (only one booking per person per event is allowed)
  • Events cancelled due to force majeure (natural disasters, government restrictions, etc.)

5. Event Cancellation by Organizer

If we cancel the event for any reason:

  • Full refund (100%) will be provided to all ticket holders
  • Refunds will be processed within 7-10 business days
  • You will be notified via email and SMS
  • Option to transfer ticket to a rescheduled date (if applicable)

6. Ticket Transfers

Tickets are non-transferable unless explicitly authorized by us. If you wish to transfer your ticket:

  • Contact us at least 48 hours before the event
  • Provide details of the new attendee
  • A transfer fee of ₹100 may apply
  • Only one transfer per booking is allowed

7. Payment Gateway Refunds

Refunds are processed through Razorpay payment gateway. Please note:

  • Razorpay may take additional time to process refunds
  • Refund initiation depends on your bank/card issuer
  • We are not responsible for delays caused by payment gateways or banks

8. Disputes

In case of any dispute regarding refunds or cancellations:

  • Contact us first at info@thinklabdigitalsolutions.com
  • We will respond within 48 hours
  • Unresolved disputes can be escalated to consumer forums

9. Changes to Policy

We reserve the right to modify this refund and cancellation policy at any time. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting on our website.

10. Contact Us

For any questions about refunds or cancellations:

Please contact us at info@thinklabdigitalsolutions.com for any refund-related queries.
