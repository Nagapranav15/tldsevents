⚠️ ALL TICKET SALES ARE FINAL

1. No Refunds Policy

TLDS Events operates under a strict NO REFUND policy. Once a ticket is purchased:

  • No refunds will be issued for any reason
  • No cancellations are permitted
  • No exchanges or transfers are allowed
  • No credits will be provided for future events

2. No-Show Policy

If you fail to attend an event (no-show):

  • Your ticket becomes void
  • No refund or credit will be provided
  • Ticket cannot be used for future events

3. Event Cancellation by Organizer

In the unlikely event that we must cancel an event:

  • Tickets may be transferred to a rescheduled date
  • Credit for future events may be issued at our discretion
  • Refund decisions are at sole discretion of management

4. Force Majeure

We are not responsible for events affected by circumstances beyond our control, including but not limited to:

  • Natural disasters, pandemics, or emergencies
  • Government restrictions or lockdowns
  • Artist/performer cancellations
  • Venue unavailability

In such cases, we will make reasonable efforts to reschedule, but no refunds will be issued.

5. Ticket Transfers

Tickets are non-transferable and cannot be sold or given to another person. The name on the ticket must match the attendee.

6. Purchase Verification

Before completing your purchase:

  • Verify the event date, time, and venue
  • Ensure you can attend before buying
  • Double-check your personal details
  • Understand that all sales are final
